## Beschrijving (uittreksel)

Over deze job As Communications & PR Coordinator, you will be a vital part of the small, but ambitious Club Paradis team. You’ll be creating content, writing texts, setting up communication and PR strategies, following up on daily press requests, and assisting on all aspects of PR, branding, and communication. We are looking for someone with a profound interest and curiosity in the arts, design and architecture and a knowledge of all things related to communication. A good knowledge of Dutch, French and English is a must, and graphical skills a plus. Because of the varied nature of the job, positivity, personality and a hands-on mentality are the skills best suited to fit this position. Club Paradis offers an exciting and varied position in an interesting and dynamic work environment. You will be able to work closely with and for the leading players in contemporary arts, design, and architecture. We welcome ambitious people with a sense of initiative and ownership. At Club Paradis we do not compromise on quality, professionalism, or fun. As a Communications & PR Coordinator your tasks will include: Content creation in Dutch, French and/or English Copywriting in Dutch, French and/or English Producing editorial content and press releases in Dutch, French and/or English Conceiving refreshing ideas and ways to integrate content Building strong stories and editorial angles Setting up social-media campaigns and influential partnerships with cultural and lifestyle partners Updating the journalist database Managing campaigns in an efficient way, according to set deadlines Producing and managing social-media content Setting up brand-building strategies Following up on daily press requests quickly and efficiently in a professional manner Analysing and reporting press coverage Required skills: A minimum of 2 years of relevant professional experience in PR, communications, media or a related field High standards of copywriting Efficient communication & marketing skills Experience in digital media, marketing, and editorial content Good spoken and written knowledge of Dutch, French, and English Sufficient knowledge of art, design and architecture A genuine interest in art, design and architecture A hands-on mentality Good organisation, a proactive attitude, and an eye for detail Good taste!
