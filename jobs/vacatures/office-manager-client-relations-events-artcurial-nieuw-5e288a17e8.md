## Beschrijving (uittreksel)

Over deze job Working closely with the Director of Artcurial Belgium, the local team and our specialists and departments in Paris, you will play a central role in the day-to-day coordination of the Belgian office. The position combines office management, client relations, events and exhibitions, communication and operational coordination, in a dynamic international art-market environment. Key responsibilities Office Coordination & Operations Ensure the smooth day-to-day running of the Brussels office. Maintain a clear overview of ongoing files, client requests, upcoming auctions, valuation days, exhibitions and key deadlines. Coordinate and follow up operational matters relating to the office, suppliers and service providers. Manage office purchases, supplies and general administrative needs. Support the Director with day-to-day organisation, priorities and follow-up. Valuation days & Specialist visits Organise valuation days and specialist visits in Belgium, from planning through to execution. Welcome and follow up with clients before and after valuation appointments. Support specialists and the local team with inventories, valuations, photographs and documentation when required. Ensure appropriate follow-up of potential consignments and ongoing client files. Exhibitions & events Coordinate and organise exhibitions, previews, vernissages, valuation events and other client events in Belgium. Handle practical organisation including invitations, guest lists, logistics, suppliers, catering and onsite set-up. Liaise with Paris departments regarding artworks, catalogues, communication materials and event requirements. Follow projects from initial planning through to the event and subsequent client follow-up. Client relations Welcome and assist clients visiting or contacting the Belgian office. Build and maintain strong long-term relationships with collectors, sellers, buyers and partners. Follow up proactively on client requests and ensure they are directed to the appropriate specialist or department. Maintain and update client information and mailing lists. Marketing & Communication Coordinate local marketing and communication initiatives with Artcurial headquarters in Paris. Follow up on newsletters, advertising, website content, invitations and other communication materials. Provide local content and information for upcoming Belgian activities. Coordinate with external partners and media contacts when relevant. Ensure consistency and quality across all client-facing communication. Partnerships & Business development support Support the development and coordination of local partnerships and collaborations. Identify opportunities to strengthen Artcurial’s visibility and network in Belgium. Assist with relationship management involving partners, institutions and other key contacts.
